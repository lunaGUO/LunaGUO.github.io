I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Texas A&M University, working with [Dr. Zheng O'Neill](https://engineering.tamu.edu/mechanical/profiles/oneill-zheng.html) in the [Building Energy and HVAC&R Research Group](https://hvac.engr.tamu.edu/).

Before joining Texas A&M, I received my M.S. in HVAC & Gas Engineering from Tongji University, where my thesis developed a hybrid energy consumption prediction model for office buildings based on heterogeneous data, advised by [Prof. Peng Xu](https://a434.tongji.edu.cn/english/GROUP/Team_leader.htm). I received my B.E. in Built Environment (with a minor in Business Administration) from Chongqing University, where my thesis explored the automatic design of HVAC systems based on Building Information Modeling (BIM).

I also worked as a *Machine Learning Research Intern* at **Hewlett Packard Enterprise**, developing reinforcement learning control for data center liquid cooling systems, and as a *Graduate Research Intern* at **Oak Ridge National Laboratory**, developing control-oriented models and conducting field tests of model-predictive control strategies for grid-interactive commercial buildings.
